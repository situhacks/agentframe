# Edit — {name}

Source `video/source.mp4` · transcript `video/transcript.json` · cut `video/cut.mp4` · render `renders/final.mp4`. Procedure and grammar: `library/process/short-form-edit.md`. Edited in place until the post is `posted`, then frozen.

## Hook (first three seconds)

The line that opens, quoted, with its transcript time. If the strongest line is not first, say which line moves up.

## Cut list

Ranges to remove with the reason (retake, filler, dead air, tangent). The compiled plan from `transcript-cut.mjs --plan` lands here before encoding.

## Punch-ins

Time · scale · the emphasized word. Three to six per minute.

## B-roll slots

Time · what he names · the `af search` query · the chosen asset path and sub-window · full-frame or picture-in-picture.

## Text cards

Time · the claim or number · card style from the design language.

## SFX and bed

Cue time · asset from `library/assets/audio/` · level. Sparingly.

## Captions

Identity from the channel's video design language; rail or burned track; any words to drop.

## End card and CTA

The closing line and the on-screen card.

## Platform caption and tags

Keyword-first first line, then the caption, then three to five tags.

## QC

What was checked: first three seconds, safe zones, duration, size, no watermark. Operator review before `scheduled`.
