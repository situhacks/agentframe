# life.md

Shapes `workspace/life/life.md`: what is true about the operator now, injected whole into every `main` session by `af hook session-start`. No verb writes it; main and the consolidator edit it by hand.

```
---
cap_lines: 200
private: true
as_of: 2026-10-25
review: operator
---
# Life

## Who I am now
## Standing commitments
## This quarter
## Subsystems
## People right now
## How I work
## Reading rules
```

- The body stays under `cap_lines`. `af doctor` reports a hard issue over the cap and a note at 90%; the bundle leads with the overage when there is one.
- Each line is a current fact with its date, or a pointer to the knowledge page that owns the detail. History belongs in `knowledge/<topic>.md` timelines and `log/`, never here.
- A fact that changes is replaced, not struck through. Cut before adding.
- `review: operator` marks a draft he has not confirmed; remove it once he has read the file.
